El Pollo Locco

🌵 The Story !

X

In this action-packed game, you play as Pepe, a fearless hero from Mexico, on a mission to save his homeland from a bizarre new threat: a horde of evil chickens taking over the desert! A strange force has turned the once-peaceful chickens into wild, aggressive beasts. Now led by the terrifying end boss, El Pollo Grande, they’ve begun to attack villages across the land. The people are desperate — and Pepe is their last hope. Armed with nothing but his courage and his abuela’s legendary spicy salsa, Pepe must journey across scorching deserts, face countless feathered foes, and put an end to the chicken invasion once and for all. Because in this world... 🔥 Only salsa can stop the madness

📱 How to Use

X

Welcome to El Pollo Locco! Here's how to control the game on your device:

🔼 Controls

Button Action
⬅️ Left (LEFT) Move character to the left
➡️ Right (RIGHT) Move character to the right
🥾 Jump (SPACE) Make your character jump
🧴 Throw Bottle (D) Throw a spicy salsa bottle

🔁 Rotate Your Device

Please rotate your device to landscape mode for the best experience. If you're in portrait mode, a message will appear automatically asking you to rotate.

❗ Notes

  • Tap and release buttons to perform actions.
  • Best played on screens wider than 720px in landscape mode.
